# Research value: calibration before an evolution fit

Pursue a bounded calibration, selection and data-access design for testing acceleration-scale evolution. Park an empirical fit until the required measurements and covariance can be verified.

The canonical design probe uses deterministic synthetic rows. It tests degeneracies between acceleration-scale evolution, baryonic calibration and measured-acceleration calibration. It does not fit galaxies or estimate survey power. A held-out synthetic test cannot remove an exact calibration symmetry.

An observational study needs a declared sample, its selection rule, covariance and calibration constraints. Stop if plausible nuisance evolution absorbs the forecast difference, or if the available data cannot support the proposed likelihood. Published curve comparisons establish prior work. Repeating them alone supplies no distinct result.

Wave 12 now traces the quoted approximately 83,900-solar-mass scale to a reproducible total-halo calculation. That mass is not a black-hole seed prediction. Later collapse requires dynamics that the calculation does not provide.
